## Group Life Cover
An employee benefit arranged by the employer that can also extend to employees' family members. "It is an annual cover and renewable."

### It is comprised of:
**Group Life Security (main cover)** – pays on death of an employee/member from natural illness or accident, 24 hours, worldwide. Benefit is payable to the Company, which pays the next of kin per the nomination form. Riders (bought separately):
- **Permanent Total Disability** – full sum assured if certified totally disabled by a qualified medical practitioner; paid to the member.
- **Critical Illness** – 30% of benefit on first diagnosis of life-threatening illness (e.g. heart attack, stroke, kidney failure, cancer); complements the limited chronic benefit in medical insurance; remaining 70% paid to next of kin on death.
- **Last Expense** – fixed amount paid to the Company within 48 hours of notification for the next of kin; improves on medical-scheme funeral benefits.
- **Free Cover Limit (FCL)** – sum assured above which medical examination is required; done by appointed providers at Kenindia Assurance Co. Ltd's cost.

### Salient features
- Arranged by employer for employees
- Benefits as multiples of annual salary or a fixed amount
- Premium per 1,000 of cover or age-based rate
- Covers death from illness and accident
- Master policy issued to the Company
- Eligibility age 18–65
- Last expense can extend to spouse and children
- Critical Illness and PTD subject to new diagnosis and a 6-month waiting period
- Members complete a Group Life application form
- Members above FCL undergo medicals at appointed providers at Kenindia's cost
- All members covered up to FCL pending medicals/underwriting

### Standard Exclusions
No benefit for death resulting from:
1. Aviation other than as a fare-paying passenger on a scheduled recognised airline (unless normal duties involve handling aircraft of a regular airline/charter service)
2. Mountaineering requiring ropes or guides (unless on duty), winter sports, big-game shooting, racing of any kind, or motorcycling
3. Influence of alcohol, illegal drugs, narcotics or medically harmful substances
4. Motorcycle racing, horseback or wheeled polo racing, winter sports – except motorcycling with engine ≤500cc
5. War, invasion, acts of foreign enemies, hostilities or warlike operations (declared or not)
6. Active participation in mutiny, riot, strikes, military/popular uprising, insurrection, rebellion, revolution, usurped power, martial law or state of siege
7. Self-inflicted injury from deliberately taken medication unless as directed by a medical professional

### Requirements
**For quotation:** employee names, occupations and payroll numbers; date of birth and age next birthday; monthly or annual basic salary; amount of cover; preferred riders.
**For effecting cover:** confirm commencement date; list of employees with salaries; completed proposal forms; Certificate of Incorporation and PIN certificate; full premium payment.
