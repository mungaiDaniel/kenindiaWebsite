# Professional Indemnity

- "The basis of the contract is the proposal form."
- "Premiums must be paid upfront before commencement of cover."
- [paraphrase] Premiums form the basis or consideration for indemnity.

## Cover

[paraphrase] "The covers caters for" all sums the insured becomes legally liable to pay to any person as compensation or damages, including the claimant's costs and expenses, "not exceeding the limits stated in the proposal form", in respect of claims or counterclaims made against the insured for negligence or alleged negligence, carelessness, omission, commission, or any act or default arising out of the business. Notice of a claim must be given to the Company during the period of insurance, or within six months after its expiry or cancellation. Kenindia will also pay litigation costs and expenses incurred with its written consent in defending a claim for compensation covered by the policy.

## Extension for Loss of Documents

[paraphrase] Covers documents entrusted to the insured, or property in the insured's custody, if found destroyed, damaged, lost or mislaid and cannot be found. The Company will indemnify against:
- [paraphrase] Legal liability the insured may incur as a result of such loss of documents.
- [paraphrase] Costs and expenses of replacing or restoring the damaged, destroyed, mislaid or lost property or documents.

[paraphrase] "Insured" means any person who, during the period of the policy, is a partner in the firm, or any person in employment (salaried or not) entrusted with and acting in good faith in the normal conduct of the insured's business or practice.

"Terms and conditions apply."
