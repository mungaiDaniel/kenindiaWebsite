# Travel Insurance

## Travel Insurance
Covers medical expenses arising from a "sudden and unexpected illness or accident" while travelling outside Kenya, with extensions:
- Personal Accident (death or permanent disability)
- Hospital benefit (fixed cash per day while hospitalised)
- Loss of checked baggage
- Delay of checked baggage
- Loss of passport
- Personality Liability
- Travel Delay
- Hijack

This is not a general health policy; it will not pay claims arising from a "pre- existing medical condition". Available only to "permanent residents of Kenya".
