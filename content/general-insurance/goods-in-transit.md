# Goods In Transit

- "The basis of the contract is the proposal form."
- "Premiums must be paid upfront before commencement of cover."
- "Premiums form the basis or the consideration for indemnity."

## Types

1. "Goods in Transit (Road Risk) Insurance."
2. "Goods in Transit (wider Risk) Insurance (widely accepted)."

## Cover

[paraphrase] Described as "All Risks" insurance: indemnifies the insured against physical loss of and/or damage to goods in transit from any accidental cause, including fire, lighting (sic — "lightning"), breakage of bridges, collision with or by the carrying vehicle, overturning of the carrying vehicle, derailment, and accidents of any nature to the carrying railway wagon or vehicle — "subject to limited specific perils."

[paraphrase] Goods are covered while being transported by road or railway, or while temporarily housed in the ordinary course of transit.

"Terms and conditions apply."
