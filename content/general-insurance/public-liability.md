# Public Liability

"The basis of the contract is the proposal form." "The consideration for indemnity being premiums." "Upfront payment of premiums is mandatory before commencement of cover."

## Cover

1. [paraphrase] Death of or bodily injury to a third party, i.e. any person other than a person employed by or acting for the insured or any sub-contractor to the insured.
2. "The consideration for indemnity being premiums." (repeated on page)
3. [paraphrase] Damage to property not belonging to, held in trust by, or under the control of the insured or their employees or sub-contractors, where the damage is not recoverable under any other policy. Defence costs are payable in addition to the amount of indemnity.
