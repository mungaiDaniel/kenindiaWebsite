# Fire Insurance

## Introduction

"This is insurance for occurring fire cases, which provides protection against loss in the event of a fire."

[paraphrase] Kenindia offers four categories: Fire Domestic Insurance, Fire Industrial (Material Damage) Insurance, Fire Business Interruption (Loss of Profits), and Industrial All Risks. Under every class the basis of the contract is the proposal form (page: "the basis of the contract are proposal forms"), premiums are the consideration for indemnity, and full premium payment is required upfront before cover starts.

## Fire Domestic Insurance

[paraphrase] Offered for residential or private dwelling houses or private flats; covers domestic outbuildings, landlord's fixtures and fittings, walls, gates and fences of the same property. Indemnity for damage up to the values stated in the proposal form, if caused by:
- "Fire, lightning, thunderbolt, earthquake or volcanic eruption."
- "Explosion."
- "Riot and strike."
- "Aircraft or other aerial devices."
- "Bursting or overflowing of water tank apparatus."
- "Theft accompanied by actual forcible and violent breaking into the building."
- "Impact to the building by any road vehicle or animal not belonging to the insured."
- "Storm and tempest."
- "Additional expenses incurred for alternative accommodation and loss of rent."

NOTE: "The Insurance therein may be extended to include contents under section B." [paraphrase] Contents may include furniture, fixtures, household goods and personal effects of the insured or family members permanently living with them.

## Fire Industrial (Material Damage) Insurance

[paraphrase] A basic Fire policy covering the insured's capital assets, which may include:
- "Buildings."
- "Stock in trade."
- "Furniture, fixtures and fittings."
- "Office equipment."

[paraphrase] Perils covered: fire, lightening (sic), "limited explosion", aircraft and other aerial devices, strike, riot and locked-up workers, malicious damage, earthquake or volcanic eruption, storm, tempest and floods, escape of water from any tank, apparatus or pipes, impact by any road vehicle or animal, bush fire, subterranean fire and spontaneous combustion.

## Fire Business Interruption (Loss Of Profits)

[paraphrase] Consequential loss cover protecting future earning capacity: covers loss of profit from reduced or stopped production following sudden and unforeseen damage, including breakdown of plant or machinery. The insured's sales (Turnover, sometimes Revenue) are what is at risk. Covers loss of profit from damage at the insured's premises or elsewhere (e.g. suppliers' or customers' premises), plus additional expenditure from increased cost of working. Perils, conditions and warranties must match the Fire material damage policy, and a business interruption claim is admissible only if the fire material damage claim is first admissible.

## Industrial All Risks

[paraphrase] A comprehensive fire policy including material damage and business interruption, normally offered considering volume of business and a large sum insured.

### SECTION A - MATERIAL DAMAGE

- "Buildings."
- "Plant and machinery spares."
- "Office equipment, fixtures, furniture's and fittings."
- "Other contents."
- "Stock in trade."
- "Customer's property."

### SECTION B – BUSINESS INTERRUPTION

- "Gross profit."
- "Increased cost of working."
- "Auditors Fees."

NOTE: "With an indemnity period ranging between 6 months to 18 months." [paraphrase] The policy may be extended to cover theft, subject to the policy's terms, conditions and warranties.
