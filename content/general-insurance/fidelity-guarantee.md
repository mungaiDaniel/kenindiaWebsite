# Fidelity Guarantee

- "The basis of the contract is the proposal form."
- "Premiums must be paid upfront before commencement of cover."
- "Premiums form the basis or the consideration for indemnity."

## Cover: Infidelity of Employees

[paraphrase] Covers loss from forgery, embezzlement, larceny or fraudulent conversion of money and/or stock in trade belonging to, or held in trust by, the insured, committed by employees in connection with their employment, as specified in the proposal form schedule, during the period of insurance. Kenindia Assurance Company Ltd will make good the loss by indemnifying the insured for the amount of the loss or the value of the stock in trade, "not exceeding in the case of each of the employee, the amount set against his or her name in the schedule."

Acts of fraud or dishonesty must be:
- "During the period of Insurance."
- "During the employee's uninterrupted service in employment."
- "Must be discovered and notified to the Company not later than six months" [paraphrase] after the employee's resignation, dismissal, retirement or death, or after termination or lapse of the policy.

[paraphrase] The insured must observe and fulfil the policy's terms, provisions, conditions and endorsements; the truth of the statements and answers in the proposal form "shall be conditions precedent to any liability".

"NOTE: Terms and conditions apply."
