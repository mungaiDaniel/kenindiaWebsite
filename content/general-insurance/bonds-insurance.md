# Bonds Insurance

[paraphrase] The contract is based on the proposal form. Premiums must be paid upfront before cover begins. Premiums "form the basis or the consideration for indemnity."

## Bond Insurance

[paraphrase] A bond is a financial guarantee that compensates third parties for loss arising from the insured's failure to perform the task described.

[paraphrase] Bond types commonly issued by insurers (page wording: "Some bonds commonly signed by insurers"):
- **"Performance Bonds"** — [paraphrase] used when a contractor fails to complete the contracted work.
- **"Immigration/Security Bonds"** — [paraphrase] issued to non-citizens whose conduct the insurer guarantees.
- **"Customs/Import Bonds"** — [paraphrase] ensure that dutiable goods on which duty has not been paid are not sold in the local market before duty is paid. (Page sentence begins "These guarantees that, dutiable goods on which duty has not been paid...")
