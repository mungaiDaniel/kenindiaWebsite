# Work Injury Benefits

[paraphrase] The basis of the contract is the proposal form. Premiums must be paid upfront before commencement of cover, and "Premiums form the basis or the consideration for indemnity."

## Cover

[paraphrase] Applies if any employee in the insured's immediate service sustains bodily injury by accident or disease "arising out of and in the cause of employment" (sic) in the insured's business as described in the proposal form. Kenindia Assurance Company Ltd, subject to the jurisdiction clause and the other terms, exceptions and conditions of the policy, will indemnify the insured against liability under the work injury benefits Act for such injury, disease or death. If the insured dies, the insurer indemnifies the insured's legal personal representatives under the policy terms.

[paraphrase] Compensation for any one occurrence, or series of occurrences from one source or original cause, is limited to the limit of liability stated in the schedule, conditional on the insured revising estimated earnings and paying any additional premium; otherwise compensation is limited to the last period's earnings and it will be deemed that the work injury benefits Act had remained unaltered.

"Terms and conditions apply."
