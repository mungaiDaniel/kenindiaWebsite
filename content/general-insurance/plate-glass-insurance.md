## Motor Commercial (as shown on this page)

"Under motor commercial insurance, all private vehicles are covered. The covers given are classified into three levels as below:"

### Third party

[paraphrase] Covers the owner's or driver's legal liability to third parties for injury to persons or damage to their property, subject to exclusions.

### Third party fire and theft

[paraphrase] Everything under Third party, plus fire and theft of the vehicle and/or its accessories, subject to exclusions.

### Comprehensive cover

[paraphrase] Third party, fire and theft, plus accidental and malicious damage. The insured is indemnified for loss of or damage to the vehicle, its accessories and spare parts "whilst thereon":
- "By accidental collision or overturning subsequent upon mechanical breakdown."
- "By fire, external explosion, self ignition, or lightning or break in or theft."
- "By malicious acts."
- "By accidents caused by or through loading or unloading of vehicles (Goods carrying vehicles) by road, rail, inland waterway lifts or elevators."

[paraphrase] Loss may be paid in cash, by reinstatement, or by repair of the motor vehicle or any part or its accessories. Liability should not exceed the market value of the vehicle, the value of the accessories, or the sum insured, whichever is lower.

[paraphrase] Comprehensive cover, subject to agreement, attracts extensions at no extra premium:
- "Radio Cassette."
- "Windscreen."

"In any case, the insurance contain an imposed excess." "An excess is the first part of any claim which is borne by the insured."

[paraphrase] Kenindia Assurance Company Ltd extends cover to "Motor PSV Self Drive", "Motor PSV Private Hire" and "Motor PSV General Cartage Insurance" for valued clients.

"Terms and conditions apply."
