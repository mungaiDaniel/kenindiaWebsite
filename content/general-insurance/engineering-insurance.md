# Engineering Insurance

## Introduction

[paraphrase] Engineering is described as the most technical branch of accident insurance. It arose after frequent boiler explosions damaged nearby property and caused injuries or deaths. Cover is accepted on condition that boilers and machines are regularly inspected, since inspection is part of the insurance. The proposal form is the basis of the contract. Premiums are paid upfront before cover starts. Indemnity is governed by the contract's terms, conditions and exclusions.

Accident Insurance Products:
- Contractors All Risks Insurance
- Erection All Risks Insurance
- Machinery Breakdown Insurance
- Boiler & Pressure Plant Insurance
- Electronic Equipment Insurance
- Deterioration of Stock Insurance
- Engineering Business Interruption Insurance

## Contractors All Risk Insurance

[paraphrase] Covers civil engineering works, from small buildings to large dams, against causes such as fire, water, storm, impact and internal defects. Exposure starts at delivery of materials to the site, continues during construction and extends into the maintenance period after handover.

Perils covered during construction (per contract requirements):
- fire and lightning
- accidental damage during construction
- water damage
- floods
- storm or tempest
- collapse
- collision
- impact
- aircraft
- burglary, theft and malicious damage
- damage due to defects in materials, design or workmanship
- riots, strikes
- landslide
- earthquake

[paraphrase] The insured reviews the contract price (Sum Insured) during the policy period to keep pace with inflation and replacement cost.

### COVERAGE OF THE POLICY

- [paraphrase] Material damage section: covers items lost, damaged or destroyed by any cause; loss is made good by repair or replacement, "subject to of each and every item" (sic).
- [paraphrase] Third-party section: covers legal liability for accidental damage to property and for fatal or non-fatal injury to any person, up to the limits in the schedule.
- [paraphrase] Legal costs recovered by a claimant, and costs incurred by the insured "with the consent of insures" (sic), are covered, subject to an excess for third-party damage claims (no amount stated).

## Erection All Risks Insurance

[paraphrase] Covers erection of electrical plant, machinery and structures involving little or no civil works; the manufacturer or supplier is responsible until handover to the owner. Includes third-party claims for property damage or bodily injury arising from the erection project. Sum insured is the new replacement value, including freight, customs dues, and erection and testing costs.

- "By accidental collision or overturning subsequent upon mechanical breakdown."
- "By fire, external explosion, self ignition, or lightning or break in or theft."
- "By malicious acts."
- "By accidents caused by or through loading or unloading of vehicles (Goods carrying vehicles) by road, rail, inland waterway lifts or elevators."

## Machinery Breakdown Insurance

[paraphrase] Covers sudden and unforeseen physical loss or damage to machines while working, at rest, or during cleaning, inspection, overhauling or relocation within the premises. Covers boilers with auxiliaries, electrical equipment, alternators and generators, mechanical plant (engines, turbines, pumps) and lifting equipment (cranes, lifts). The insured regularly reviews the sum insured to reflect new replacement value, including installation, customs, freight, repair and specialist costs.

### DAMAGES COVERED

- Faulty design, material, erection or construction faults at the workshop.
- Short circuit, failure of insulation and other electrical causes.
- Faulty operation, lack of skill, negligence or malicious acts.
- Tearing apart due to centrifugal forces.
- Failure of connected machinery or protective devices.
- Falling impact or collision.
- Obstruction or entry of foreign objects.

## Boiler and Pressure Plant Insurance

[paraphrase] Also called Boiler Explosion Insurance. Covers explosion or collapse of boilers and pressure vessels during ordinary working, indemnifying against:
- Damage to the insured's boiler and/or other pressure plant.
- Damage to the insured's surrounding property.
- Legal liability to third parties for fatal or non-fatal bodily injury.
- Damage to third-party property.

## Electronic Equipment Insurance

[paraphrase] Covers computer systems, peripherals and auxiliary equipment against loss or damage, in two sections:
- Material damage: loss or damage from accidental causes.
- Consequential loss: follows an admitted material damage claim and covers increased cost of working (e.g. hire of alternative facilities, loss of business).

### COVER

[paraphrase] Sudden and unforeseen physical loss or damage requiring repair or replacement, including electrical and mechanical damage, impact, negligence, theft and burglary, fire and special perils. Items covered:
- Electronics Data processing (EDP) Equipment
- Electronic equipment for medical use
- Telecommunication and navigational equipment
- Computer system for production plant & machinery
- Signal and transmitting units
- Equipment for research and material testing

### SUM INSURED

[paraphrase] Replacement cost, including installation, transport and customs duties. Covers costs during the indemnity period to keep data-processing operations at their previous level.

## Deterioration of Stock Insurance

[paraphrase] Covers deterioration of goods in cold storage caused by breakdown of refrigeration machinery.

### METHODS OF PRESERVATION

[paraphrase] Freezing conditions with a controlled atmosphere ("22% carbon dioxide, 4% oxygen and 74% nitrogen") and normal atmosphere. Insureds may be owners, users or tenants, including meat or fish processing and food preservation businesses. The cover complements machinery breakdown insurance and is written "on accommodation basis."

### COVER

- Rise or fall in cooling temperatures.
- Unforeseen and sudden escape of refrigerant into cold storage rooms.
- Under-controlled storage or incorrect atmosphere composition causing material damage to refrigeration plant indemnifiable under machinery breakdown.
- [paraphrase] May be extended to cover deterioration from public power failure, provided public and back-up supplies are satisfactory.

## Engineering Business Interruption Insurance

[paraphrase] Covers actual loss of gross profit from business interruption caused by an event covered under machinery breakdown insurance (sudden and unforeseen damage, including breakdown, to plant and machinery), addressing high repair costs and resulting business losses.

### COVER

- Loss of Profits
- Continuing business expenses (standing and fixed costs)
- Increased cost of working (additional expenditure reasonably incurred to avoid reduced turnover)
