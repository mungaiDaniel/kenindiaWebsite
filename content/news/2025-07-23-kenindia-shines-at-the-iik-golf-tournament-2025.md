# Kenindia Shines at the IIK Golf Tournament 2025

Kenindia Assurance demonstrated exemplary performance at the IIK Golf Tournament held on 4th July 2025 at the Royal Nairobi Golf Club. Competing on a challenging par-70 course, Mr. Keith Wanasakaami, the Assistant Manager, New Business Life Department, secured second place overall with a commendable 44 Stable ford points.

Furthermore, he was honoured with the "2nd Nine Winner" Trophy, awarded for the best score on the back nine holes, as well as the **"Division 4 Overall Winner" Trophy**, recognizing the best player in his division. This achievement reflects Kenindia's unwavering commitment to excellence, discipline, and teamwork, whether on the greens or in business.
