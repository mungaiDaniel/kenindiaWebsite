# Kenindia Assurance Delivers Sustainable Water Solutions at Akuisi Comprehensive School

Students and staff at **Akuisi Comprehensive School** in Nakuru County struggled to access clean water. Children walked long distances, losing class time; the school faced frequent hygiene-related illnesses, unsafe meal preparation and the cost of trucking in water. Girls often missed lessons because of poor sanitation and menstrual-health challenges.

Kenindia Assurance, in partnership with **CorpsAfrica/Kenya**, now provides reliable clean water to over **650** students, staff and residents (Bahati, Rongai, Nakuru County). Safe water in the school compound has reduced illness, improved the school feeding programme, supported a new kitchen garden, and freed students from fetching water so they can focus on studies. More than **200** nearby households and farmers also use the supply.

For Kenindia, the project shows how targeted interventions can improve health, restore dignity and create lasting opportunities for the next generation.
