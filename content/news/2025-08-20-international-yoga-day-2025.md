## One Earth, One Health through Yoga

On **21 June**, the world marked the International Day of Yoga, observed annually since its adoption by the United Nations in 2014, under the theme "Yoga for One Earth, One Health." The High Commission of India in Nairobi hosted the 11th edition at the **Sikh Union Club Grounds**, drawing over **2,500** participants from government, diplomatic missions, the UN, instructors, students and wellness groups.

The event presented yoga as a holistic practice building strength, posture, flexibility and circulation, with mental benefits including stress reduction, emotional resilience and inner calm through breathing and meditation – a form of preventive healthcare. Kenya's participation reflected its wider commitment to wellness.

KenIndia was recognised for its contribution: Managing Director **Mr. Yogesh Meshram** received a special appreciation from the High Commission of India for his role in planning and delivering the event.

The post closes by framing well-being as part of KenIndia's responsibility alongside insurance, supporting community initiatives that build healthier, more resilient communities.
