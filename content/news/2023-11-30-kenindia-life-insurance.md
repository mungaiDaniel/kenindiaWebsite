Life insurance (life assurance) pays a set amount – the sum assured – to a beneficiary when the insured person dies. The policy may run for a fixed term or for the insured's whole life.

## Important Terminologies in Life Insurance
- **Insured** – the person whose life is covered.
- **Sum insured/assured** – the cover amount in Kenya Shillings.
- **Policy term** – fixed (e.g. 20 years), to a certain age, or whole of life.
- **Premium** – monthly, quarterly, semi-annual or annual payments.
- **Policy document** – the contract with all terms and conditions.
- **Beneficiary** – person named to receive the benefit on death.
- **Bonuses** – added to some policies and paid at maturity; guaranteed or performance-based.
- **Surrender value** – cash available on some policies, usually after at least three years of premiums.

## Type of Life Insurance Policies
Three main types of ordinary life insurance in Kenya:

### Endowment Life
Most common in Kenya. Fixed term, often with partial maturities and a final payout; sum assured paid on death during the term. Bonuses accrue; surrender value after three years. Example: an education policy.

### Whole Life
Runs to a set age (e.g. 65). Sum assured plus bonuses paid on death or at maturity. Bonuses and surrender value after three years.

### Term Life
Pure protection – no bonuses, surrender value or maturity payout. Example: credit life (clears a loan balance on death); premiums usually included in loan repayments.

### Supplementary Coverage/Riders
1. **Accidental Death Rider** – extra 150–200% of sum assured on accidental death.
2. **Critical Illness Rider** – stated % of sum assured on diagnosis of a specified critical illness.
3. **Permanent and Total Disability Rider** – extra 150–200% of sum assured.
4. **Waiver of Premium Rider** – future premiums waived on a named event (e.g. PTD).

## Do You Need a Life Insurance Policy?
1. Do you have dependants?
2. Do you have assets?
3. Would your dependants be financially secure if you died, or would they need to sell what you leave behind?
4. Do you want a forced savings plan (school fees, wedding, land, retirement)?

If you answer yes to at least three, seriously consider a policy.

### How Much Does it Cost?
Premiums depend on: 1) your age; 2) your general health (medical exams typically required above **Kshs 5 million** sum assured); 3) the sum assured.

To buy, complete an application form; an agent advises on the premium; once accepted you receive a policy document.
