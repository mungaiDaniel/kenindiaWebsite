# KenIndia's Annual Agents Convention Dinner

> **EXTRACTION NOTE:** Firecrawl was rate-limited and direct HTTP to kenindia.com is blocked from this environment, so this body was captured via WebFetch, which would not return verbatim text. Below is a CLOSE PARAPHRASE preserving paragraph order, names, numbers, dates and short exact quotes (in quotation marks). Copy the exact wording from WordPress (Posts > edit) before going live.

The Life Department hosted its Annual Agents Convention Dinner at the "prestigious Nairobi Serena Hotel", celebrating the top brokers and agents whose dedication drove the company's results over the past year.

Attendees included Managing Director & CEO **Yogesh Meshram**, Chief Operating Officer **Irene Owiti**, and General Managers **V.S Madhu** (Life Division) and **Babu Rajan** (General Division). Guest of Honour **Leah Gatonye**, Manager, Consumer Protection, spoke on the importance of "ethical conduct and client trust."

The highlight was the awards ceremony honouring agents and brokers for sales, consistency and service excellence, and for helping secure clients' financial futures. The gala set an ambitious tone for the coming year.

The evening reinforced KenIndia's commitment to strong client relationships and service excellence. "Long Live KenIndia!"
