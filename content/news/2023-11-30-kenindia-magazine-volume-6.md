Editors' note for the sixth edition. As business process re-engineering reached high standards and ISO certification preparation continued "into 2016", staff worked intensively on processes, systems, documentation and corrective actions, clarifying work processes and showing strong team spirit across offices. The editors congratulate staff on now being ISO certified and list ongoing activities: training, fire drills, monthly prayer meetings, new Life and General business software, CSR activities and Board meetings.

Project results depend on leadership; leaders drive activities to completion, and leadership is closely tied to mentoring. The magazine team chose **"Leadership"** as the theme, including a mentoring article by **Gidraff Macharia**.

The editors wish readers well and invite feedback and ideas via the magazine's email address. – Kenindia Magazine Team
