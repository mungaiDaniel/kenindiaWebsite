# A Call for Love, Support, and Inclusion

Below is a CLOSE PARAPHRASE preserving paragraph order, names, numbers, dates and short exact quotes (in quotation marks). Copy the exact wording from WordPress (Posts > edit) before going live.

On 25th March, World Cerebral Palsy Awareness Day, Kenindia Assurance Company Limited partnered with the **Noweza Foundation** on a CSR initiative combining compassion, awareness and community action. Its core message: no child should be left behind.

The event stressed that children with cerebral palsy have two parents and that care should be shared equally. It acknowledged that some fathers leave after learning of a child's condition, leaving mothers to carry the emotional, physical and financial load alone, and urged fathers to step forward, stay present, and show their children love and dignity.

Beyond awareness, the event offered practical help. Through a **diaper drive**, Kenindia donated essential supplies to mothers caring for children with cerebral palsy, many of whom struggle to access basic necessities. "The relief and gratitude on their faces spoke volumes."

The campaign also called on the wider community to treat care for these children as a shared responsibility, and to build environments where every child is accepted, understood and able to thrive.

Kenindia and the Noweza Foundation did more than raise awareness: they started conversations, restored hope, and showed that empathy and collective effort can build a more inclusive society. "Because every child matters. And every parent matters too."
