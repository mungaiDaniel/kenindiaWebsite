# KenIndia Assurance Executives Toast Coastal Success

> **EXTRACTION NOTE:** Firecrawl was rate-limited and direct HTTP to kenindia.com is blocked from this environment, so this body was captured via WebFetch, which would not return verbatim text. Below is a CLOSE PARAPHRASE preserving paragraph order, names, numbers, dates and short exact quotes (in quotation marks). Copy the exact wording from WordPress (Posts > edit) before going live.

KenIndia hosted an exclusive dinner and cocktail event at **Sarova Whitesands Beach Resort** to honour its agents, brokers and valued clients in **Mombasa**, celebrating recent business success and continued partnership.

Senior leadership attended: Managing Director & CEO **Yogesh Meshram**, Chief Operations Officer **Irene Owiti**, and General Managers **Babu Rajan** (General Division) and **V.S Madhu** (Life Division) – underlining the importance of the coastal region to the company's strategy.

The evening moved from cocktails to dinner and was focused on recognition, thanking partners for their ongoing support. Success in Mombasa was described as a shared achievement; the event strengthened key relationships and set a collaborative direction for the future. "Cheers to continued growth! Long live KenIndia!"
