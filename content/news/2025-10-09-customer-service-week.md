# Customer Service Week

> **EXTRACTION NOTE:** Firecrawl was rate-limited and direct HTTP to kenindia.com is blocked from this environment, so this body was captured via WebFetch, which would not return verbatim text. Below is a CLOSE PARAPHRASE preserving paragraph order, names, numbers, dates and short exact quotes (in quotation marks). Copy the exact wording from WordPress (Posts > edit) before going live.

Customer Service Week is a reminder that "our customers are the reason we exist."

For over four decades Kenindia has grown alongside families, businesses and communities across Kenya. Customers' stories of resilience, loyalty and feedback drive continuous innovation and better service.

The company's success rests on customer confidence; that loyalty motivates it to uphold professionalism, reliability and integrity.

Looking ahead, customers remain at the centre of Kenindia's vision – its greatest strength, partners and family.

Thank you to all policyholders, partners and supporters for choosing Kenindia Assurance; the company pledges to remain a dependable provider of protection, peace of mind and support at every stage of life.
