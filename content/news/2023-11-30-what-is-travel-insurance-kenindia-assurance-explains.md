Interview (originally by Juma, Soko Directory) with **Subhash Deshkulkarni**, General Manager, Kenindia Assurance.

Trip planning involves many decisions, but some risks are beyond a traveller's control.

## What is Travel Insurance?
"Travel Insurance is an insurance product that provides cover to policyholders when they travel overseas." People travel for family visits, holidays, study, work or business, and things can go wrong – lost luggage, emergencies abroad or illness.

## What does it cover?
- Personal accident cover for injury or death (compensation to next of kin on death)
- Dental and optical cover
- Daily hospital cash if bed-ridden
- Trip cancellation
- Lost or delayed luggage
- Compensation for hostage, hijacking or wrongful detention, based on days inconvenienced

Policies run from 1 to 180 days.

## How many kinds of travel insurance are there?
About five: **Premium, Business, Schengen, Youth** (up to 28) and **Incoming** (for those coming to Kenya). Frequent travellers can take an annual cover.

## Schengen travel insurance
Several European countries (e.g. Finland, Slovakia, Greece, Iceland, Hungary, Norway) require minimum Schengen travel insurance for visas. Deshkulkarni says Kenindia is the only insurer offering this product.

## How does cover work abroad?
Before travel, Kenindia gives the traveller a contact number for **Europ Assistance**, which can arrange emergency evacuation, hospital monitoring, legal assistance and repatriation of mortal remains.

## Are children covered?
Yes – children under 12 can share a parent's policy when travelling with the parent.

## Are there exclusions?
Typically: loss/theft of unattended baggage or cash, self-inflicted injuries, civil unrest, pre-existing medical conditions.

## Is travel insurance expensive?
Generally affordable; premiums vary with cover type, age, destination, length of stay and pre-existing conditions.
