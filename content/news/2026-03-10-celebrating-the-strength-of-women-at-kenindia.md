# Celebrating the Strength of Women at Kenindia

Below is a CLOSE PARAPHRASE preserving paragraph order, names, numbers, dates and short exact quotes (in quotation marks). Copy the exact wording from WordPress (Posts > edit) before going live.

At Kenindia Assurance Company Limited, International Women's Day 2026 was a chance to pause and recognise the women whose commitment, resilience and passion shape the organisation every day.

The company hosted a special breakfast for its women staff, giving colleagues a relaxed setting to connect and celebrate the different paths each woman brings to the workplace.

The room was filled with laughter, open conversation and shared stories. The event showed how mutual support, and spaces where women feel "seen, heard, and valued", build strength.

This year's theme, "#GiveToGain – Advancing Rights, Justice, and Action for All Women and Girls," was a reminder that progress depends on sharing opportunities and encouraging every woman to grow, lead and make an impact.

Women at Kenindia play an important role across departments and leadership levels; their creativity, commitment and determination drive the company forward.

As the breakfast ended, the central message was that when women lift each other up, workplaces, communities and future generations all benefit.

Kenindia views celebrating women as an ongoing commitment, not a one-day event.
