# Dynamic Advantage

## Description
"DYNAMIC ADVANTAGE PLAN" is an endowment life cover. On completion of the policy/premium payment term "the maturity value along with bonus/profits became payable" to the policyholder. Loans may be available depending on how long premiums have been paid. Life cover runs during the premium-paying term and ends when the maturity benefit is paid.

Riders allowed:
- Waiver of Premium
- Double Accident Benefit
- Joint Life Cover
- Level Term Benefit
- Critical Illness Cover
- Inflation Sensitive Benefit
