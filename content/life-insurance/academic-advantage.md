# Academic Advantage

## Description
Life insurance policy covering the parent. Maturity instalments are paid during the last four years of the policy term; the policy term is chosen by the customer.

| Payout point | % of Sum Assured |
|---|---|
| Last but three years | 20% |
| Last but two years | 20% |
| Last year | 30% |
| Completion of term | 50% & Bonus |

## Death Benefit
On death of the parent, the full Sum Assured plus accrued bonuses is paid, regardless of instalments already paid.

## Riders
- Waiver of Premium
- Double Accident Benefit
- Joint Life Cover
- Level Term Benefit
- Critical Illness Cover
