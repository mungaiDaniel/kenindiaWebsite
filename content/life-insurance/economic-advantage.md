# Economic Advantage

## Description
"ECONOMIC ADVANTAGE PLAN" is a whole life plan: life cover runs "throughout the lifetime or up to the person's attaining age 99." "The premium paying term can be chosen by the customer." Cover "extends beyond the premium payment term" and the bonus "accumulates throughout the lifetime of the policyholder." The life assured may also pay premiums "throughout the lifetime."

Riders allowed:
- Waiver of Premium
- Double Accident Benefit
- Joint Life Cover
- Level Term Benefit
- Inflation Sensitive Benefit
- Critical Illness Cover
