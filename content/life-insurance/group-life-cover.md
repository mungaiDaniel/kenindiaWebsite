# Group Life Cover

<!-- Headings below other than Group Life Security, the three riders and Free Cover Limit were unlabelled blocks on the live page; labels added for structure. -->

Employer-arranged employee benefit covering employees (and family members). Annual, renewable. Eligibility 18–65 years. Covers death from illness and accidents. A master policy is issued in the Company's favour. Rated per 1,000 of cover or by age-based rate; benefits set as multiples of annual salary or a fixed amount. Members complete a Group Life application form.

## Group Life Security
Main cover on the member's life for death from natural illness or accident, 24-hour basis, "worldwide geographical coverage". Benefits are paid to the Company for onward payment to next of kin per the nomination form. Riders available: Permanent Total Disability, Critical Illness, Last Expense.

## Permanent Total Disability
Full Sum Assured paid to the member if certified totally disabled by a qualified medical practitioner. 6-month waiting period; new diagnosis.

## Critical Illness
30% of the benefit paid on first diagnosis of life-threatening illness (e.g. heart attack, stroke, kidney failure, cancer); complements ("compliment") the limited chronic benefit in medical insurance. Remaining 70% paid to next of kin "incase of death arising". 6-month waiting period; new diagnosis.

## Last Expense
A specified amount paid to the Company within 48 hours of notification for onward payment to next of kin. Improves on funeral benefits in the medical scheme. Can be extended to spouses and children.

## Free Cover Limit (FCL)
Sum assured threshold above which medical examinations are required, done at appointed providers and paid by Kenindia Assurance Co. Ltd. Members are covered up to the FCL until underwriting is complete.

## Benefits to the Company
1. Improved staff retention, reduced hiring costs.
2. Better reputation through securing employees' families.
3. Higher employee morale and performance.
4. "tax relieve" – premiums are an allowable expense before tax.
5. Claim payments are made in full and are not taxed.

## Our Responsibilities
- Keep member records updated.
- Pay claims within 10 working days.
- Start renewal 60 days before renewal date.
- Hold regular service meetings.
- Handle technical and administrative scheme matters.
- Train and assist members on scheme information.

## Exclusions
No benefit is payable for death resulting from:
1. Aviation other than as a fare-paying passenger on a scheduled recognised "Air Line" (exception for those whose duties involve aircraft).
2. Mountaineering with ropes or guides (except on duty), winter sports, big game shooting, racing, motorcycling.
3. Being under the influence of alcohol, illegal drugs or other harmful substances.
4. Motorcycle racing, polo or winter sports, except riding a motorcycle of 500cc or less.
5. War, invasion, hostilities, warlike operations.
6. Active participation in mutiny, riot, strike, uprising, rebellion, martial law or "stage of siege".
7. Self-inflicted injury from medication unless taken as directed by a medical professional.
