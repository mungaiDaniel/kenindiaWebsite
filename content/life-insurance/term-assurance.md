# Term Assurance

"TERM ASSURANCE PLAN" – a pure risk cover that "covers the life risk during the premium paying term"; "no maturity value is payable on completion of the term".

- Offered at "very low premium rates"; premiums "usually payable for a specified term of years or until death occurs."
- "The proposer is required to pay the medical examination fees."
- "No surrender, loan or paid up values will be granted under this policy."
- "Persons over the age of fifty are not eligible for this insurance plan."
- Minimum Sum Assured: "Kshs 1,000,000.00"
- "NO ride is allowed in this plan."
