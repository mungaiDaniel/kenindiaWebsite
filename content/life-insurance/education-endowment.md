# Education Endowment

Money-back plan paying instalments during the final five years of the term, as a percentage of the Sum Assured.

| Payout point | % of Sum Assured | |
|---|---|---|
| Last but 4 years | 15% | |
| Last but 3 years | 15% | |
| Last but 2 years | 15% | |
| Last but 1 years | 15% | |
| Completed term | 50% | & Bonus |

- "the premium waiver benefit is in-built for disability and death of parent": on death of the parent further premiums are waived and the policy continues with the same benefits.
- Monthly benefit: "on 1% of the sum assured is payable monthly" until the end of the policy term.
- "in-built accidental loss of limbs benefit" for the child: twice the Sum Assured.
- Reimbursement of medical expenses up to 25% of the Sum Assured.
