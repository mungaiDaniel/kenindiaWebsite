# 15 Years Money Back

## Description
- Guaranteed payout of 15% of the Sum Assured every three years; 100% of the Sum Assured paid at maturity.
- Cover on the full Sum Assured applies throughout the plan period.
- Entry age: 18 to 45 years, subject to underwriting.

## Benefits of Being a Policy Holder/Investor
- Periodic payouts of 15% every three years.
- At the end of the 15-year term: 100% of the Sum Assured plus accrued vested bonus and a final additional bonus.
- Part payments already made are not deducted from the maturity or death benefit.
- Regular payouts help policyholders plan their budgets.
- Combines savings, investment and life cover.
