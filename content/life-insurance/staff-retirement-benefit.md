# Staff Retirement Benefit

## Staff pension scheme/fund
- Periodic pension payments on retirement.
- "Up to one-third of the of the total accrued benefits" may be taken as a lump sum at retirement; the balance buys an annuity/pension.
- Early leavers: deferred pension or lump sum.
- On a member's death, benefits are payable to dependants.

## Staff provident scheme/fund
- Provides a lump sum to employees on early leaving, retirement or death (paid to legal heirs on death).
- The most flexible retirement scheme: members may take the full accrued benefit as a lump sum or use it to buy an annuity/pension.

## Basic features of both schemes/funds
- Early leavers: deferred pension or lump sum.
- On death "benefits are become payable" to beneficiaries/dependants.
- Tax Benefits & Contributions.
