# Kumi Bora

## Kumi Bora with profit
For persons wanting protection, saving and investment for family, with "lump sum benefit at periodical intervals" as survival benefit.

- Survival benefits: 10% of Sum Assured in the 4th year, 10% in the 6th year, 10% in the 8th year.
- Maturity (end of "10years" term): "100% of the Sum Assured plus accrued bonus + terminal bonus", giving "an extra 30% of the Sum Assured."
- "Full sum assured is payable on death during the term, over and above payments already made."
- Riders: "D.A.B. & W.O.P" (Double Accident Benefit, Waiver of Premium).
- "extended non- medical facility."
