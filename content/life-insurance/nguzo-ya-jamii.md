# Nguzo ya Jamii

A "stand alone funeral expense product" covering financial obligations "when we are alive and after our demise"; Kenindia also helps with "part of the education of the children."

## Who is eligible for this product?
- Principal member: 18–60 years. Spouse: 18–60 years.
- Children: maximum 4, aged 1 month to 21 years.
- Optional: principal member's 2 parents and 2 parents-in-law, maximum entry age 80.
- All members covered under one policy with the principal member.

## What is the allowable term?
5 or 10 years.

## How much is the life cover?
"KES.50, 000", KES 100,000, KES 250,000 or KES 500,000. The principal member's choice applies to all members.

## How much is the premium?
Depends on sum assured, age and number of members. Payable monthly, quarterly, half-yearly or yearly.

## Is there any rider available?
No separate rider; "inbuilt waiver of premium" on death of the principal member during the term.

## Death Benefit
- Principal member: full Sum Assured plus 30% of Sum Assured as an education grant.
- Spouse, parents/parents-in-law, children: full Sum Assured.
- Documents: written intimation letter (cause and date of death), burial permit, copy of deceased's ID / birth certificate / passport.
- Maximum five claims per policy year.
- After the principal member's death, premiums for spouse and up to four children are waived and the policy runs to term end; cover for parents/in-laws and excess children ceases (oldest first).
- No surrender value.

## What happens if there is no claim throughout the policy term?
30% of total premiums paid is returned to the principal member as a survival bonus.

## Is there a claim waiting period?
- 6 months: principal member, spouse, children. 12 months: parents and parents-in-law.
- Accidental death covered with no waiting period; deaths from illness during the waiting period are not paid.

"CLAIM PROCESSED WITHIN 48 HOURS ON RECEIPT OF FULL DOCUMENTATION."
