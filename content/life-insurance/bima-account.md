# Bima Account

Risk-plus-return life plan combining life cover with investment growth.

## Who is eligible to take this cover?
"Any person between the ages of 18 years to 60 years can avail this cover." Maximum maturity age 70.

## How does it work?
- Single deposit or five annual instalments. Minimum target KES 100,000 (KES 20,000 × 5, or single KES 100,000).
- Premiums, net of charges, go into a separate policyholder account.

## Can one top up?
Yes, in multiples of KES 10,000 at any time during the term, without a new policy.

## How much is the return on investment?
Growth is declared yearly and compounded. Declared return for 2022: 11% (rates vary).

## What is the available term/duration?
10, 15 or 20 years.

## How much is the life cover?
A multiple of the premium, capped at KES 500,000 per policy and KES 1,000,000 per life. Premium charges for the cover are deducted from the fund on each policy anniversary.

## Death Benefit
Full Sum Assured plus the fund balance is paid to the beneficiary/nominee.

## Can one get a loan?
After two years, up to 80% of the accumulated amount, at 3% above the last declared rate.

## Is there early withdrawal?
Discouraged, but allowed after two completed policy years.
