# Anticipated Whole Life

## Description
This plan has "combined the benefits of the whole life and anticipated endowment plans": money-back instalments during the premium-paying term, then whole-life cover on the full Sum Assured after the premium-paying term; bonuses accumulate from maturity.

Premium-paying terms: 9, 12, 15, 20 or 25 years.

| Term | Instalments (% of Sum Assured, policy year) |
|---|---|
| 9 | 25% (yr 3), 25% (yr 6), 50% (yr 9) & Bonus |
| 12 | 25% (yr 4), 25% (yr 8), 50% (yr 12) & Bonus |
| 15 | 25% (yr 5), 25% (yr 10), 50% (yr 15) & Bonus |
| 20 | 20% (yr 5), 20% (yr 10), 20% (yr 15), 40% (yr 20) & Bonus |
| 25 | 15% (yr 5), 15% (yr 10), 15% (yr 15), 15% (yr 20), 40% (yr 25) & Bonus |

## Riders
(Available only during the premium-paying term)
- Double Accident Benefit
- Critical Illness Cover
