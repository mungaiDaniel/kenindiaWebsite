# Christmas Bonus

"A unique plan, which gives guaranteed income every year for Christmas Celebration at the rate of 2.5% of the Sum Assured."

## Salient Features
- Annual bonus of 2.5% of the Sum Assured, payable on 15th December every year until death, even after the policy matures.
- At maturity, 50% of the Sum Assured is payable.
- After maturity the policy continues as a free paid-up policy for 50% of the Sum Assured, payable on death only.
- On death during the term, the full Sum Assured is payable; Christmas bonuses already paid are not deducted.

## Example of Benefits
- A man aged 32 takes a 25-year policy for "KShs. 100,000/-". Bonus is "KShs. 2,500/=" per year (2.5%), payable on 15 December until his death.
- The first bonus is payable only if 6 months have passed since the first premium was received and the policy is in full force.
