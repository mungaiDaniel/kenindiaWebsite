# Capital Advantage

"Kenindia's Capital Advantage Plan gives you unbeatable advantage over other investment plans." A parent or grandparent deposits a single lump sum with a child as beneficiary; the company invests the funds and adds yearly earnings to the capital.

## How is the amount deposited?
Single lump sum.

## Who is the beneficiary?
Children from birth to age 16. Policy matures at age 21; term 5 to 21 years.

## What is the rate of return?
11% declared after the 2022 valuation; future returns will vary.

## What is the life cover?
Automatic cover on the child from age 11, at three times the initial deposit, capped at KES 250,000 per policy. Parents may optionally add cover with a medical report. Premiums are deducted from interest earned (nothing paid up front).

## Who can propose/purchase the policy?
Parents; grandparents (who may name grandchildren as beneficiaries).

## Early withdrawal?
No penalty after 5 years; earlier withdrawals may incur costs to recover company expenses.
