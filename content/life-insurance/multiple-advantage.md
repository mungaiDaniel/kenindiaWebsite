# Multiple Advantage

## Anticipated Endowment Plan for Periodic Money-Back Installments
"MULTIPLE ADVANTAGE PLAN" – policy terms of 12, 15, 20 or 25 years. Money-back instalments, as a percentage of the Sum Assured, are paid during the term.

| Policy Term | 1st Installment | 2nd Installment | 3rd & Final Installment | |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 12 | 4th year 20% | 8th year 20% | 12th year 60% | & Bonus |
| 15 | 5th year 20% | 10th year 20% | 15th year 60% | & Bonus |
| 20 | 10th year 20% | 15th year 20% | 20th year 60% | & Bonus |
| 25 | 15th year 20% | 20th year 20% | 25th year 60% | & Bonus |

Full Sum Assured is payable on death of the life assured "regardless of the installments received earlier."

Riders allowed:
- Waiver of Premium
- Double Accident Benefit
- Joint Life Cover
- Level Term Benefit
- Critical Illness Cover
