## Our Beginning

Kenindia Insurance Ltd "was established as merger of Indian Insurance Companies operating in Kenya to form a vibrant joint venture" "on 6th December 1978" "with moral and financial support from leading local business elite".

"With a steady growth and strong share holding over a period of 45 years, the year ending 2022 KENINDIA closed at Ksh 11.1 Billion in Gross Written Premium"

"Since then, Kenindia has grown from strength to strength to become a leading household name in Kenya."
